Statistical Reasoning & Measurement

The architecture of inference: population versus sample, parameter versus statistic, and why a well-drawn sample licenses a claim about a whole. Levels of measurement, validity and reliability of measures, and the distinction between descriptive, inferential and predictive questions.

  • Inductive vs deductive logic
  • Nominal · ordinal · interval · ratio
  • Operationalisation
  • Sources of bias

📊 Chapter 2 Checking…

Descriptive Statistics & Visual Communication

Measures of centre, dispersion and position — mean, median, trimmed mean, variance, standard deviation, coefficient of variation, quantiles and z-scores — with skewness and kurtosis as descriptors of shape. Data cleaning, outlier rules, and the grammar of honest graphics: which chart answers which question, and which distort.

  • Chebyshev & empirical rule
  • Box plots · IQR fences
  • Skewness · kurtosis
  • Chart integrity

🎲 Chapter 3 Checking…

Probability & Probability Distributions

Axioms, conditional probability, independence and Bayes' theorem. Random variables, expectation and variance; the binomial, Poisson, hypergeometric and geometric models for counts; the uniform, exponential and normal models for measurements, with the normal approximation and continuity correction.

  • Bayes' theorem
  • Binomial · Poisson
  • Normal & standard normal
  • E(X) · Var(X) · linear combinations

🎯 Chapter 4 Checking…

Sampling Design, Sampling Distributions & the CLT

Probability designs (simple random, systematic, stratified, cluster) against non-probability designs and the bias each invites. The sampling distribution of the mean and proportion, standard error, the finite-population correction, and the central limit theorem — stated precisely, with its conditions and its failure cases.

  • Standard error
  • Central limit theorem
  • Stratified vs cluster
  • Sampling distribution of p̂

📏 Chapter 5 Checking…

Estimation & Confidence Intervals

Point and interval estimators, unbiasedness and efficiency, and the estimate ± multiplier × SE skeleton. z- and t-intervals for a mean, Wilson and Wald intervals for a proportion, χ²-intervals for a variance; sample-size determination for a target margin, and what a confidence level does and does not claim.

  • t distribution · df
  • Margin of error
  • Sample-size formulas
  • Interval for σ²

⚖️ Chapter 6 Checking…

Hypothesis Testing: One-Sample Tests

The test as argument by contradiction: null and alternative, tail selection, critical regions, p-values and the α–p decision rule. Type I and Type II error, power and effect size; z- and t-tests for a mean, the z-test for a proportion, the χ²-test for a variance, and the duality between tests and confidence intervals.

  • p-value vs α
  • Type I · Type II · power
  • Effect size (Cohen's d)
  • Test–interval duality

👥 Chapter 7 Checking…

Comparing Two Groups

Independent versus paired designs and the single question that decides between them. Pooled and Welch t-tests, the F-test for equal variances, the paired t-test on differences, the two-proportion z-test, confidence intervals for a difference, and the confounding trap in observational comparisons.

  • Welch vs pooled t
  • Paired differences
  • Two-proportion z
  • Confounding

🔬 Chapter 8 Checking…

Analysis of Variance

Why multiple t-tests inflate the family-wise error and how ANOVA partitions variation — SST = SSB + SSW — into an F ratio. One-way ANOVA with Tukey HSD and Bonferroni follow-ups, two-way ANOVA with main effects and interaction, assumption checks (Levene, normality of residuals), and η² as effect size.

  • F ratio · MSB/MSW
  • Tukey HSD · Bonferroni
  • Interaction plots
  • Levene's test

🧮 Chapter 9 Checking…

Chi-Square Tests for Categorical Data

Observed against expected counts and where the degrees of freedom come from. Goodness-of-fit, the test of independence in an r × c table, the test of homogeneity, the expected-frequency rule (fₑ ≥ 5), Yates' correction, and Cramér's V and the odds ratio as measures of association strength.

  • Goodness-of-fit
  • Independence · homogeneity
  • Cramér's V
  • Odds ratio

📈 Chapter 10 Checking…

Correlation & Simple Linear Regression

Covariance, Pearson's r and Spearman's ρ, with Anscombe's quartet as a warning to plot first. Least-squares estimation of the line, inference on the slope, r² and the standard error of estimate, confidence versus prediction intervals, residual diagnostics, and the difference between correlation and cause.

  • Pearson r · Spearman ρ
  • Least squares
  • t-test on β₁
  • Residual plots

🧠 Chapter 11 Checking…

Multiple Regression & Model Diagnostics

Holding the other predictors constant: partial slopes, adjusted R², the overall F-test and individual t-tests. Dummy coding of categorical predictors, interaction terms, multicollinearity and the variance inflation factor, heteroscedasticity, influential points, and principled model selection.

  • Partial coefficients
  • Adjusted R² · F-test
  • Dummies · interaction
  • VIF · influence
